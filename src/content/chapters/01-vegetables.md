subtitle: how it started

My daughter was three and would not eat vegetables. One night, instead of negotiating, I made her a storybook. I used AI to write it and draw it. She was the hero, and the vegetables were part of the adventure. She cleared her plate.

I made books for friends' children next. Parents kept asking for the same two things: a character who looked like their kid, and a story from their own culture. No bookstore had either. That was the moment personalized books stopped being a trick and became a product.
