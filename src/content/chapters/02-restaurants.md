Before any of that, I spent six years as a backend engineer, most of them at Rakuten in New York. I led the backend for Rakuten Dining, which brought more than ten thousand U.S. restaurants into the rewards program and opened a new line of revenue.[^1] I also ran a proof of concept that moved one service to serverless and cut its infrastructure cost by 90 percent. I showed the numbers to the executives, the company adopted the approach everywhere, and overall infrastructure costs fell by 80 percent.

At Serverless Inc. I rebuilt billing on Stripe. Processing time fell by 98 percent and errors by 95 percent.[^2]

[^1]: Node.js, Express, Redis, Postgres, AWS. One API served both the iOS and Android apps.
[^2]: Node.js, TypeScript, DynamoDB, AWS Lambda, Stripe API.
