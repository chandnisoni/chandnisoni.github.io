Now I am building ThinkMocha's second product. Edgar Miner reads every SEC filing so you don't have to. It turns filings into structured data and maps the relationships between companies, for people who do investment research. It is a prototype, and it is in progress: it ingests the SEC's archive back to 1995 and is learning to answer questions about who is connected to whom.[^4]

In 2026 I also built Fundraising Agent, which runs a founder's investor outreach with a human approving every send.

[^4]: Django, Postgres, Dagster pipelines, XBRL extraction, and a graph layer.
