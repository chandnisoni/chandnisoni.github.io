ThinkMocha LLC is my company. I started it in 2022, and its first product was the storybook platform. I built all of it alone: the research with parents and teachers, the product, and the pipeline that writes a story and illustrates it.[^3] A preschool in Manhattan ran a pilot with us. We delivered more than fifty books, and more than nine in ten of the educators and parents said they liked them.

Then I went to NYU Stern for the Tech MBA, to learn the parts of company-building I did not have yet. While there I sat on the screening committee at Endless Frontier Labs, reading deep-tech startups the way I now read filings.

[^3]: Next.js, Express, and generative models for text and illustration.
