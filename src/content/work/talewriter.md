Four of the books. Each one was written and illustrated for one child: their face on the cover, their city, the thing they love that week. In a Manhattan preschool pilot we made 50+ of them, and 90%+ of parents and teachers asked for more. Talewriter is ThinkMocha's first product; the current one is [Edgar Miner](/work/edgar-miner/).
