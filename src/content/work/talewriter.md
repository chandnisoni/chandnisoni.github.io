Children's books are written for an average child who does not exist. A parent looking for a hero who looks like their kid, or a story from their own culture, mostly finds nothing. And a three-year-old who will not eat vegetables does not care about the average child at all.

Talewriter makes a book for one child. Their face, their name, their city, their grandmother's food, and the thing they need a story about this week: vegetables, a new sibling, the first day of school. A book made for one child, at the price of a book made for a million.

It started at my kitchen table with my daughter and a plate she would not touch. In a Manhattan preschool pilot we made 50+ books, and 90%+ of parents and teachers asked for more. The idea is bigger than a company. In a generation, the book where you are the hero will be the default, not the gift.

Talewriter is ThinkMocha's first product. The current one is [Edgar Miner](/work/edgar-miner/).
