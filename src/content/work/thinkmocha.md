## The problem

Children's books are written for an average child who does not exist. A parent looking for a hero who looks like their kid, or a story from their own culture, mostly finds nothing. And a three-year-old who will not eat vegetables does not care about the average child at all.

## What I built

A platform that writes and illustrates a book for one specific child. The parent describes the child and what the story should be about; the pipeline writes the story, generates consistent illustrations of that character across pages, and lays out a printable book.

- Story generation tuned for read-aloud length and age.
- A character that stays recognizable from page to page.
- Culturally specific settings, names, and foods, by request.
- Print-ready layout.

## How it's built

Next.js front end, Express backend, and a generative pipeline for text and illustration. I did the parent and teacher research, the product, and the engineering alone.

## Where it is

Paused. We ran a pilot with a preschool in Manhattan, delivered 50+ books, and 90%+ of the educators and parents gave positive feedback. ThinkMocha LLC continues; its second product is [Edgar Miner](/work/edgar-miner/).
