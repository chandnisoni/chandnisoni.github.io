Raising a round is a second full-time job. Founders spend 40+ hours a week on it, and the work comes back around: a few months after one round closes, the next one starts. Most of those hours go to questions that have answers somewhere in their own contacts. Which funds fit my stage and sector? Who writes checks of the size I need? Who has money to deploy right now, and who just spent it? Which of my connections can get me a warm intro?

Fundraising Agent answers those questions from the founder's own data. You describe the company and the round, upload your LinkedIn connections, and it shortlists the funds that fit and says why: stage, sector, recent activity. For each fund it builds a dossier, the thesis, the partners, the likely objections, the hook for a first email. It drafts that email into your Gmail for you to send, logs the meetings, finds the first-degree path to a partner, and each morning hands you the next actions: send this draft, follow up with that fund.

The founder keeps the judgment and the relationships. The agent keeps the list. A founder's time belongs on the product, not on the raise.
