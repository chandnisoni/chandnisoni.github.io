Raising a round is a second full-time job. Founders spend 20 hours a week on it, and most of that time goes to questions that have answers somewhere in their own contacts: who in my network, or my network's network, can get me to the right investor? Who writes checks of the size I need? Who actually has money to deploy right now? Which warm connection has gone cold since the last round?

Fundraising Agent answers those questions from the founder's own contacts and inbox, and then does the follow-through. It finds the path to each investor through people you already know, drafts the intro ask or the cold email, sends from your Gmail once you approve, replies, follows up on silence, and keeps every warm connection warm from one round to the next. Each morning it hands the founder the five things that would move the raise today.

The founder keeps the judgment and the relationships. The agent keeps the list. 20 hours a week becomes under 2 hours.
